Khidki forwards SMS you choose — including everything, or narrower OTP and alert categories — to up to five people you configure. Forwarding runs only while you start a window (15 minutes to 2 hours, or until you stop).

Nothing leaves the phone except messages you allow. There is no internet permission, no account, and no cloud.

Features:
• People list with per-person filters (Shopping, Banks, UPI, Government, alerts, All SMS, named senders)
• Start / stop from Home
• Until I stop duration
• Ongoing notification with Stop forwarding
• SMS only (not RCS)

Not on Google Play. GitHub Releases APKs are signed by the developer; F-Droid builds are signed by F-Droid. The two cannot update each other in place.

Sideloaded SMS apps may get a Play Protect warning. That is Google’s sideload heuristic, not hidden tracking. Prefer F-Droid or adb install for testers you trust.

Never use live bank OTPs when testing.
